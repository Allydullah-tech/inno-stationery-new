Features

Everything your shop needs, in one place

Replace notebooks and scattered spreadsheets with one organised system your whole team can use.

Sales

Record sales in seconds, track every item sold and review daily totals at a glance.

Stock control

Know exactly what you have. Manage products, categories and prices with live quantities.

Purchases

Log new stock manually or import from Excel, with a full breakdown of every purchase.

Credit & debts

Keep track of customers who owe you, record payments and never lose a balance again.

Damages

Record damaged or lost items so your stock and profit figures stay honest.

Expenses

Capture rent, transport, utilities and more, so you see your real profit.

Services

Photocopy, printing, binding — manage your non-stock services alongside products.

Reports

Clear sales, profit and stock reports you can filter by date and export when needed.
