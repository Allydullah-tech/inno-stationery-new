Add products to stock

Every item added here increases stock immediately and updates its buying cost.

Upload a CSV with a header row. The system recognizes common column-naming variations, so your own spreadsheet's headers usually work as-is:
• Product name: product_name, product, item, or name
• Quantity: quantity, qty, or units
• Buy price (cost per unit): buy_price, amount, unit_price, or cost
• Selling price (optional, only used for new products): new_sell_price or selling_price
Buy price must be the cost per single unit — if your supplier quoted a price per dozen or box, divide it by how many units are inside first. Matches products by exact name, or creates a new one if none exists.
